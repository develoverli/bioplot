# bioplot — product

## One line

Tell a Chainers farmer what to plant right now to bank the most biopoints before the next
reward pools close.

## Who it is for

Active Chainers Farm players who already understand plots, lamps and reward pools, and who
are trying to squeeze more out of an inventory they already own. Not a beginner's guide, not
a wiki, not a marketplace.

## The job

> "I have these plots and these seeds. What do I plant, and how much is that worth?"

Every screen exists to answer that. Anything that does not move that answer forward is out.

## Principles

1. **The answer first.** Totals sit above the fold; the inputs that produced them sit beside
   them. A player who changes one number sees the consequence without scrolling.
2. **Show the arithmetic.** Per-harvest, per-hour and banked-in-window are all displayed,
   because they disagree and the disagreement is the insight. Never a single opaque score.
3. **Honest over impressive.** Rounded growth times go *up*. Unrecognised data is reported,
   never guessed. Known-suspect numbers are called out in the README rather than hidden.
4. **Nothing leaves the machine.** No account, no server, no telemetry. The tool is worth
   less than a player's account safety.
5. **Usable with zero install.** The extension is a convenience. Manual entry is a first-class
   path, not a fallback for the unlucky.

## Scope

**In:** seeds, plot rarities, phytolamps, lands, the 24h schedule, the seed ranking table,
local inventory import, manual entry, export.

**Out for now:** animals and crafted feed, fertilizer timing, merge planning, market prices,
tier progression forecasting, anything that writes to the game.

## Success

A player opens it, sees a plan that beats what they were doing, and can explain *why* it wins.
